# Warranty Policy

## Standard Coverage
All products purchased directly from the company come with a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

## What Is Covered
- Manufacturing defects present at time of purchase
- Component failure occurring under normal, intended use
- Battery capacity dropping below 70% of original within the first year (for battery-powered devices)

## What Is Not Covered
- Accidental damage (drops, liquid exposure, cracked screens)
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Normal wear and tear (scratches, fading, cosmetic wear)
- Damage caused by use outside the product's intended purpose
- Products with removed or altered serial numbers

## Extended Warranty
Customers may purchase an extended warranty (2 additional years) within 30 days of the original purchase date, which adds coverage for one incident of accidental damage per year.

## Claim Process
1. Customer provides proof of purchase and a description of the issue.
2. Support determines whether the issue is likely a covered defect or requires physical inspection.
3. If inspection is required, a prepaid shipping label is provided.
4. Once received, the item is repaired, replaced, or refunded (company's discretion) within 10 business days.

## Warranty Transferability
The standard warranty is tied to the original purchase and is not transferable to a second-hand buyer unless the extended warranty plan was purchased, which is transferable one time.

## Regional Variations
Warranty terms may differ in the EU and UK due to local consumer protection laws, which mandate a minimum 2-year warranty regardless of the standard policy stated above. Support should check the customer's region before quoting warranty length.
