# Shipping Policy

## Processing Time
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days of being placed. Orders placed after 2 PM local time are processed the following business day. Processing does not occur on weekends or public holidays.

## Shipping Methods and Timeframes
| Method          | Estimated Delivery Time | Cost                  |
|-----------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| Standard        | 5-7 business days       | $4.99 (free over $50) |
| Expedited       | 2-3 business days       | $12.99                |
| Overnight       | 1 business day          | $24.99                |
| International   | 7-14 business days      | Calculated at checkout|

## Order Tracking
Once an order ships, a tracking number is automatically emailed to the customer. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear after the label is generated.

## Delayed Shipments
If a package has not moved for more than 5 business days after the expected delivery window, support should first check the carrier's tracking page for exceptions (weather, customs, address issues) before escalating. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, a replacement or refund is issued.

## Address Errors
Orders shipped to an incorrect address provided by the customer are not eligible for a free reshipment. If the error was on the company's part (wrong label, system error), a free reshipment is issued immediately.

## International Orders
International customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees imposed by their country. These are not included in the checkout price and are collected by the carrier or customs agency upon delivery.

## Split Shipments
Orders containing items from multiple warehouses may arrive in separate packages with separate tracking numbers. This is not an error and does not require escalation unless a package is confirmed missing.
