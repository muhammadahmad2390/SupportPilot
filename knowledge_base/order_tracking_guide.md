# Order Tracking Guide

## Order Status Definitions
- **Pending**: Order received, payment not yet confirmed.
- **Processing**: Payment confirmed, order is being prepared for shipment.
- **Shipped**: Order has left the warehouse and is with the carrier.
- **Out for Delivery**: Package is on the final delivery vehicle for the day.
- **Delivered**: Carrier has marked the package as delivered.
- **Delayed**: Carrier has flagged an exception (weather, customs, missed scan).
- **Returned to Sender**: Package could not be delivered and is being sent back.

## Common Tracking Issues

**Tracking number shows no updates**
This is normal for the first 24-48 hours after a label is created, as the carrier has not yet physically scanned the package. If no update appears after 3 business days, treat it as a delayed shipment.

**Status says "Delivered" but customer says they didn't receive it**
1. Confirm the delivery address matches what's on file.
2. Ask the customer to check with neighbors or building management.
3. Wait 24 hours, as carriers occasionally mark packages delivered up to a day early.
4. If still missing after 24 hours, file a carrier claim and offer a replacement or refund.

**Package stuck in transit at the same location for multiple days**
Usually indicates a sorting facility delay or customs hold (international). Support can share the carrier's estimated resolution time but should not promise a specific delivery date.

## Escalation Thresholds
- Domestic shipments: escalate to a carrier claim if no movement for 5+ business days past the estimated delivery date.
- International shipments: escalate if no movement for 10+ business days, since customs holds are common and usually self-resolve.

## What Support Can and Cannot Do
Support can reissue tracking links, file carrier claims, and offer replacements/refunds per policy. Support cannot alter a package's physical route, expedite carrier handling, or guarantee a specific redelivery time.
