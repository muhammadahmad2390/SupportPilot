# Frequently Asked Questions

## Ordering

**Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?**
Orders can be modified or canceled only within 1 hour of placement, before they enter processing. After that, the order cannot be changed and must go through the standard return process once delivered.

**Can I combine multiple orders into one shipment?**
No. Orders placed separately, even minutes apart, are processed and shipped independently and cannot be merged.

**Do you offer price adjustments if an item goes on sale after I buy it?**
Yes, within 7 days of purchase. Customers can request the price difference be refunded as store credit.

## Payments

**What payment methods are accepted?**
Major credit/debit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. Some regions also support local payment methods (e.g., Klarna in the EU).

**Why was my payment declined?**
Common causes include insufficient funds, incorrect billing address (must match the card issuer's records), or the bank flagging the transaction for fraud review. Customers should contact their bank first.

**Is it safe to save my card on file?**
Yes. Card details are tokenized and stored via a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor; the company itself never stores raw card numbers.

## Account & Access

**I can't log into my account. What should I do?**
Support should verify the email on file, guide the customer through the password reset flow, and check whether the account was flagged for suspicious activity (which would require identity verification before reset).

**Can I have multiple shipping addresses saved?**
Yes, up to 5 addresses can be saved per account and selected at checkout.

## Discounts & Promotions

**Can promo codes be combined?**
No, only one promo code can be applied per order unless explicitly stated otherwise in the promotion's terms.

**My promo code isn't working. Why?**
Check for expiration date, minimum purchase requirements, category exclusions, or single-use-per-customer restrictions before escalating.
