# Return Policy

## Eligibility Window
Customers may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. Items marked as "Final Sale" are not eligible for return under any circumstances.

## Condition Requirements
Returned items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories, manuals, and tags. Items showing signs of use, damage, or missing components may be refused or subject to a restocking fee of 15%.

## Non-Returnable Categories
- Perishable goods
- Gift cards
- Digital downloads or software licenses once activated
- Personalized or custom-made items
- Undergarments and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)

## Refund Method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. Store credit is offered as an alternative if the original payment method is no longer valid.

## Late Returns
Returns initiated after the 30-day window but within 45 days may be accepted at the discretion of support staff, issued as store credit only, minus a 10% restocking fee.

## Damaged or Defective Items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, the customer is not responsible for return shipping costs, and a full refund or free replacement is offered regardless of the standard 30-day window, provided the claim is made within 14 days of delivery.

## How to Initiate a Return
1. Customer contacts support with the order number and reason for return.
2. Support issues a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number.
3. Customer ships the item back using the provided prepaid label (for defective items) or at their own cost (for standard returns).
4. Refund or replacement is processed once the item is received.
