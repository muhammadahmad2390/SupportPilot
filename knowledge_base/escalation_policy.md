# Escalation Policy

## When to Escalate to a Human Agent
- Customer explicitly requests a human representative
- Refund or replacement value exceeds $200
- Suspected fraud or account security compromise
- Legal threats, chargebacks, or formal complaints
- Repeated failed resolution attempts (2+ back-and-forths without progress)
- Issues involving injury or product safety concerns

## Escalation Tiers
- **Tier 1 (Automated/Agent-handled)**: Order status questions, standard returns within policy, basic troubleshooting, FAQ-type questions.
- **Tier 2 (Senior Support)**: Refunds outside standard policy windows, warranty disputes, shipping issues requiring carrier claims.
- **Tier 3 (Management)**: Legal complaints, safety incidents, fraud investigations, high-value disputes.

## Response Time Expectations
- Tier 1: Immediate (automated) or within 1 business hour
- Tier 2: Within 4 business hours
- Tier 3: Within 24 hours, with an acknowledgment sent immediately

## Information Required Before Escalating
1. Order number or account identifier
2. Summary of the issue and prior resolution attempts
3. Any relevant policy exceptions already offered
4. Customer's desired outcome (refund, replacement, explanation, etc.)

## Do Not Escalate For
- Standard questions already answered in the FAQ or policy docs
- First-time requests that fall within stated policy (these should be resolved directly)
- General product usage questions unrelated to an order issue
